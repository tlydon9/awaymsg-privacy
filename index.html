AwayMsg — Privacy Policy

Last updated 26 September 2026

AwayMsg lets you write one styled away message and read your buddies'. This page says what it stores, who can see it, and how to get rid of it. It is short because the app does not do very much.

What it stores

  • Your email address, because that is how you sign in.
  • A screen name, which you choose and can change.
  • A first name and last initial, which you type in at sign-up.
  • What you write: your away message and its styling, your about-me, your chosen face and colour theme. Away messages you replace are kept so that the hall of fame can still show one people liked.
  • Who you are buddies with, who you have blocked, and which messages you have liked.
  • When you were last active, so buddies can see whether you are online.
  • A device token, if you ever turn on notifications. This build does not have notifications enabled, so it does not collect one.

That is the complete list. There is no analytics, no advertising identifier, no tracking of any kind, and nothing is shared with anyone except the database that runs the app. If you save your password on the sign-on screen it is stored in the iOS keychain on that device only — it is never sent anywhere, never synced to iCloud, and never restored onto another device.

Who can see it

  • Your email address is never shown to anyone.
  • Your screen name, face, theme, about-me, real name and away messages can be read by anyone signed in to AwayMsg who can open your profile.
  • You can turn that off. Edit profile → Privacy hides you from search and from the public boards, so only your buddies can find you.
  • Somebody you have blocked cannot see you, and you cannot see them.
  • A message you report is shown to a moderator, exactly as it was when you reported it.

Deleting it

Your profile's Account window has a Delete account button. Your profile and everything on it disappears from the app immediately. For 30 days you can sign back in and change your mind; after that it is permanently deleted, including your away messages and any record of the screen names you have used.

Where it lives

AwayMsg stores everything in a Supabase database. Supabase is the only third party involved; there are no others.

Children

AwayMsg is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect anything from them.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top changes with it.
